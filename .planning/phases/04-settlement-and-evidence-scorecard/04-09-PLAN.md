## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Backtest admission -> BullMQ | Operator-supplied plan identity becomes bounded asynchronous work. |
| BullMQ -> PostgreSQL/orchestrator | Redelivery, reordering and hostile identifiers can mutate durable receipts or consume excessive resources. |
| Historical result/forecast -> comparison | Source facts cross into performance evidence where leakage or cohort substitution could create false claims. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-04-09-01 | Spoofing | backtest queue payload | high | mitigate | Strict DTO parsing plus durable planId/planHash equality before any claim or forecast work. |
| T-04-09-02 | Tampering | window forecast/result/score linkage | high | mitigate | Exact fixture/cutoff/result IDs, existing append-only settlement constraints, immutable score IDs and transactional completion. |
| T-04-09-03 | Repudiation | retry and comparison receipts | medium | mitigate | Correlation IDs, deterministic job/window identities, policy/config hashes and retained failure codes. |
| T-04-09-04 | Information Disclosure | worker errors | low | accept | Stable bounded error codes are returned; raw provider/database payloads remain only in internal logs. |
| T-04-09-05 | Denial of Service | backtest queue/plan bounds | high | mitigate | Existing span/window/concurrency limits, bounded attempts/backoff, queue retention limits and unrecoverable rejection. |
