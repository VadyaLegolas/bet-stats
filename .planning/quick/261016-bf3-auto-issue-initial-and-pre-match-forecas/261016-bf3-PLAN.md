Algorithm:

   j. Counters: fixtures = fixtures examined; issued = jobs actually run; skipped = due-and-missing fixture-kind instances NOT issued this tick (plan deferred them or no source SyncRun/evidence), so they retry on a later tick.
   k. Finish with { event: "forecast.sweep.completed", fixtures, issued, skipped, durationMs }.
