## Cross-Phase Integration & E2E Flows

1. **Discovery to Detail (Phase 1 → Phase 5):**
   Upcoming fixtures are discovered with canonical identities, display provider provenance, and reflect provider degradation state without broken layouts or zero-substitution.
2. **Replayable Features to Forecast (Phase 2 → Phase 3):**
   Chronological team features, weighted form, and rest days correctly feed into frozen forecast snapshots at strict timestamps without future data leakage.
3. **Workbench to Manual Odds Value (Phase 3 → Phase 4):**
   Users enter positive decimal odds, inspect multiplicatively de-vigged market probabilities, and generate exact linked value receipts based on frozen forecasts.
4. **Settlement to Scorecards (Phase 4 → Phase 6):**
   Completed fixtures resolve through versioned settlement rules; settled forecasts update Brier/Log Loss and reliability diagrams across competitions and models.
5. **Provider Resilience & Operator Recovery (Phase 5 → Phase 6):**
   Exhausted quotas and provider degradation enter fail-closed states; operators can preview and execute durable transactional replays.
6. **Privacy Boundary & Complete Erasure (Phase 6):**
   Personal betting history is opt-in only, linkable records are deleted upon withdrawal or expiration, and unmodeled migration artifacts are completely dropped.

## Tech Debt & Residual Notes
